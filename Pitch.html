Growth Strategy

The problem is not simply acquisition. It is value-to-payment conversion.

Evidence

A persistent conversion constraint

Across 13 months, trial-to-paid conversion remained between approximately 1.87% and 2.08%, averaging 1.99%.

PM Decision

Fix monetization before buying more traffic

Test whether the existing trial base converts more effectively after users experience personalized financial value.

Product bet: Move the monetization moment after demonstrated value rather than asking users to subscribe while they are still setting up.
Activation

Define Aha as a decision outcome—not an onboarding task.

Setup

Connect one account

Collect only the minimum real financial data required to generate the first useful cash-flow model.

Insight

Reveal a 30-day cash-flow risk

Turn connected business data into a personalized forecast with a clear financial consequence.

Aha

Review one recommended action

Value becomes tangible when FinWise helps the user understand what is likely to happen and what to do next.

Connect Account → See Cash-Flow Risk → Review Recommended Action → Monetization
Engagement

Give users a reason to return when their financial situation changes.

Mechanic

Cash Plan Freshness

A plan remains Current until new financial information materially changes the forecast. It then becomes Needs Review and surfaces one relevant action.

PM Judgment

Natural cadence over artificial engagement

No streaks or unnecessary daily prompts. FinWise should earn the return visit because the user's financial situation changed.

Measurement Strategy

Measure the path to value—not just product activity.

Leading Signal 1

Primary account import

Measures whether users clear the first prerequisite for personalized financial value.

Leading Signal 2

Personalized action review

Measures whether users reach the candidate Aha behavior the strategy intends to monetize.

Business Outcome

Trial-to-paid conversion

Tests whether value realization ultimately translates into commercial impact.

Aggregate modeling usage showed only a weak relationship with paid conversion: r = 0.26. The strategy therefore shifts measurement toward the actual user-level journey.
Experimentation

Test whether payment performs better immediately after Aha.

Method

50/50 user-level A/B test

Randomize users immediately after personalized_cashflow_action_reviewed.

Primary Outcome

Trial-to-paid conversion

Evaluate conversion among randomized eligible users rather than optimizing for prompt clicks.

Decision Threshold

≈20% relative lift vs control

The business target is to move overall conversion from the 1.99% baseline toward at least 2.4%.

Guardrail

Protect continued engagement

Seven-day cash-plan review must not decline by more than 5% relative to control.

Decision discipline: read the result after users complete the reverse-trial period. Do not redefine success or stop because early movement looks positive.
Monetization

Charge for continuity of value—not discovery of value.

Recommended model: keep the subscription + reverse trial, but package the paid proposition around Cash Plan Continuity.
Trial Value

First realized insight

Connect one account, receive a 30-day forecast, identify a cash-flow risk, and review one recommended action.

Paid Value

Ongoing Cash Plan Continuity

Automatic forecast refreshes, material cash-buffer alerts, recurring action reviews, and additional connected accounts.

PM trade-off: validate the correct value boundary before testing discounts, price increases, or more complex packaging.
Strategic Recommendation

Build one connected growth system around the value moment.

Qualified Intent → Fast Activation → Personalized Aha → Monetization Test → Recurring Value
Core product principle: users should pay to continue receiving value they have already experienced—not to discover whether that value exists.
